PaymentsSovereignty.com

Descriptive .com domain for the emerging doctrine of “payments sovereignty”: strategic autonomy, governance and resilience of payment infrastructures, schemes, rails and wallets in the digital age.
PaymentsSovereignty.com is reserved as a neutral, non-vendor banner for a potential observatory, framework or research hub dedicated to payments sovereignty in Europe and beyond.
The focus is strictly on infrastructure, governance and resilience – not on offering payment services, wallets, accounts, APIs, advisory or compliance solutions.
Intended for central banks, supervisors, public institutions, industry consortia, payment processors, financial groups and think tanks that may wish, in the future, to operate a public-facing resource on payments sovereignty under a clear, descriptive label.

Terminology and alias

In public debates, both “payments sovereignty” and “payment sovereignty” are used. This site intentionally adopts the plural PaymentsSovereignty.com to reflect ecosystems and infrastructures rather than a single instrument. The domain PaymentSovereignty.com can be used purely as an alias and redirected permanently (HTTP 301) to this primary banner, so that both expressions converge on the same neutral observatory.

PaymentsSovereignty.com est réservé comme bannière neutre et non commerciale pour un éventuel observatoire, cadre d’analyse ou hub de connaissances dédié à la souveraineté des paiements, en particulier en Europe.
